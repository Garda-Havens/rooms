Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This page explains in plain words which personal data we collect when you visit www.gardahavens.com, contact us or stay with us, why we do it and what your rights are. It is the notice required by Articles 13 and 14 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Who handles your data

The data controller is Alessandro Barbazeni, who runs “Garda Havens” (CIN IT023022C2FWABW3P7), Via Belvedere 10, 37014 Castelnuovo del Garda (VR), Italy.

For any privacy question you can email us at garda.havens@gmail.com or call +39 378 443 7585.

2. What data we collect

  • Availability request on the website: first and last name, email, phone number, arrival and departure dates, number of rooms, adults and children, and anything you write in the notes field.
  • Direct contact: the data you send us by email, WhatsApp or phone.
  • Your stay: the guests’ identity document details required by Italian law, and the data needed for payment, receipts or invoices.
  • Browsing: to show you the pages, the technical services we use (hosting, fonts and icons) receive data such as your IP address and browser type. We do not use analytics or profiling tools.

Please do not put information in the notes field that is not needed for your booking. If you tell us about specific needs, for example allergies or accessibility requirements, we will only use them to prepare your stay.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your request and send you an offer: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To manage your booking and stay: performance of the contract (Art. 6.1.b).
  • To meet legal obligations (Art. 6.1.c): reporting guests to the Police through the Alloggiati Web portal (Art. 109 TULPS), the municipal tourist tax, tourism statistics, tax and accounting duties.
  • Specific needs you choose to tell us: based on your consent (Art. 9.2.a), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Website security and abuse prevention, such as spam on the form: our legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).

We do not use your data for newsletters or advertising, we do not sell it and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Tools we use and who receives your data

We handle your data ourselves, using password-protected electronic tools. To do so we rely on a few providers, who process it on our behalf or as independent controllers for their own services:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script): receiving and storing the requests sent from the website.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: only if you choose to write to us or be contacted on WhatsApp. The internal alerts we receive on our phone for a new request do not contain your name or contact details.
  • GitHub (website hosting), Google Fonts (fonts), Cloudflare / cdnjs (icons) and 1sticket (some images in the guidebook): they receive the technical browsing data needed to show you the pages.
  • Public authorities (Police, Municipality, statistics bodies, tax authorities), when required by law.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. In these cases the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Requests that do not become bookings: for as long as useful to reply and handle any follow-up contact, then we delete them.
  • Bookings: for as long as needed to manage your stay; receipts and tax records for 10 years, as required by law.
  • Identity document details: we send them to the Police as required and do not keep copies longer than necessary.

6. Cookies

The website does not use profiling cookies or visitor analytics, so we do not ask for cookie consent. The technical services listed above may only use the data strictly needed to display fonts, icons and images.

7. Children

The form is meant to be filled in by an adult. For children we only ask how many there are; for the stay, the data required by law is provided by a parent or guardian.

8. Your rights

At any time you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use, receive a copy in a readable format or object to its processing (Arts. 15–22 GDPR), within the limits of what the law requires us to keep. Just email garda.havens@gmail.com: we will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is not handled correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority in your country.

9. Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
